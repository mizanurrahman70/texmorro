## Notes

- Format: JPG, quality ~82, progressive. Keep each file under ~350 KB.
- Keep faces and products centred; the site crops with `object-fit: cover`.
- Do not add text, logos, or watermarks inside the photos.
- Send originals larger than the target sizes whenever possible.
